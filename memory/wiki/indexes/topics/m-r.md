# Topic Index: m-r

- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-company-overview]] — Renvoo is an early-stage Dutch healthtech startup building an operational layer for no-show prevention, confirmation, rescheduling, and appointment recovery, with a current wedge in private dental clinics.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-blog-compliance-and-safety-layer-april-2026]] — On April 24, 2026, Renvoo turned blog-content restrictions into an enforced compliance layer with policy files, deterministic validation, blocked persistence on violations, and aligned automation prompts.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-compliance-and-data-boundaries]] — Renvoo is being positioned as non-clinical logistics software that minimizes data exposure by using administrative scheduling data only, keeping the clinic as controller and Renvoo as processor.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-detection-and-modeling]] — Renvoo's modeling work exists, but the durable current stance is that prediction should stay operational, explainable, and based on non-clinical administrative data rather than borrowed clinical-style features from public datasets.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-event-outreach-spring-2026]] — Renvoo's current outreach plan for spring 2026 is to use direct dental outreach plus KNMT and NVM association routes for clinic access, while using MedtechMeeting, 4TU.Health, and Innovatiecafe mainly for introductions rather than direct clinic selling.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-founder-dental-outreach-campaign]] — Renvoo now has a founder-led dental outreach campaign built around a verified 100-clinic email list, a TU/e and NVIDIA-backed founder introduction, and a ranked send queue that starts with Brainport and Brabant clinics before broader backup cities.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-go-to-market]] — Renvoo's current GTM is founder-led, direct, and operationally framed, using face-to-face outreach, targeted email and cold calls, and pilot-first conversations with clinics.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-market-and-icp]] — Renvoo's clearest current ICP is Dutch private dental clinics, sold to owners and practice managers who directly feel empty-slot revenue loss and scheduling admin pain.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-metrics-and-roi]] — Renvoo's measurement stack centers on no-show reduction, recovered revenue, utilization, admin time savings, and backfill performance, with scenario math used heavily in sales and pitch material.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-open-questions-and-risks]] — Renvoo's biggest unresolved questions concern proof, pricing, launch-channel choice, live-data model performance, integration access, and how much of the broader vision should be deferred until the dental wedge is validated.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-product-architecture]] — The current Renvoo architecture is best understood as a Connect-Detect-Confirm-Replace workflow, with lightweight data ingestion first, staff visibility, and cautious operational control around schedule changes.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-product-thesis]] — Renvoo's product thesis is that empty appointment capacity is an operational systems problem that can be reduced by combining risk scoring, targeted outreach, response tracking, rescheduling, and waitlist backfill.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-roadmap-and-proof-plan]] — Renvoo's near-term roadmap is to secure pilot clinics, prove measurable no-show reduction and recovered capacity through a lightweight integration path, then deepen integrations and expand the market wedge.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-security-hardening-patch-april-2026]] — On April 24, 2026, Renvoo patched the main audit findings by making ingest export safe-by-default, constraining URL fetches, validating Pinecone hosts before sending credentials, hardening the public site, and restoring reliable wiki generation.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-security-posture-april-2026]] — The April 24, 2026 audit found a generally small attack surface with no known dependency CVEs or committed secrets, but identified four meaningful risks: unrestricted ingest fetches, automatic outbound export of raw source content, trust of cached Pinecone hosts before sending credentials, and a public-site posture that still leaks browser metadata to Google and lacks CSP.
- [[wiki/topics/renvoo-website-seo-and-blog-engine-april-2026]] — On April 24, 2026, Renvoo's public site was rebuilt into a Dutch-first bilingual search engine that supports classic SEO, AI-search extractability, and a daily markdown blog pipeline with validation and cautious publish controls.
