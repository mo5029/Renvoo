## Why This Topic Was Chosen

Renvoo already had live Dutch posts on:

- no-show policy in dental clinics
- late cancellations in dental clinics

- appointment confirmation workflows

What was still missing was a broader Dutch article for the buyer who is evaluating the software category itself rather than a single workflow fragment. This post fills that gap by answering a more top-level question:

- what should no-show reduction software actually improve in an appointment-heavy practice?

That makes it commercially relevant to dental clinics while remaining usable for other appointment-heavy private clinics.
