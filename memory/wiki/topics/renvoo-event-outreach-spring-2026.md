# Renvoo Event Outreach Spring 2026

## Snapshot

Renvoo's spring 2026 outreach motion should stay narrow and budget-aware. The job is not to attend the most prestigious health events. The job is to create the highest number of useful conversations with practice-side operators and connector nodes who can unlock pilots.

## What to do

- Build and work a direct dental outreach list every week.
- Use KNMT and NVM-mondhygiënisten as trusted access routes into practice owners, practice managers, and oral-care operators.
- Attend 4TU.Health as a connector and partnership event.
- Request enrollment for MedtechMeeting #2.
- Watch Innovatiecafe and only attend if the final theme and speaker mix clearly map to provider operations or ambulatory care.
- Keep the National AI in Healthcare Conference as a remote-targeting list, not a physical ticket purchase.

## Why this matters

- There is no remaining dental event in this exact window that matches Renvoo's actual wedge strongly enough to be the center of the plan.
- Association and direct outreach routes are better aligned with the current dental-first strategy than beauty or skin-therapy rooms.
- The tracker is no longer empty; Renvoo now has a real first list of public targets to work from.
- The outreach sprint is now in execution, not just planning, and the current tracker batch has been fully worked through by email.
- 4TU and MedtechMeeting are leverage events that can create introductions without pretending to be clinic-heavy rooms.
- The AI conference has relevant operators and innovation leaders, but the business price is not compatible with the current budget.
