# Renvoo Open Questions And Risks

## Snapshot

Renvoo has enough conceptual depth to build, but not yet enough evidence to claim the business is working. The main risks are practical: getting partner clinics, validating the ROI with real data, choosing a compliant launch channel mix, confirming pricing, and refusing to let the bigger company vision dilute the current wedge before it wins.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` still shows zero discovery calls and zero active pipeline as of that snapshot.
- The founder summary says the biggest need is proof that clinics will adopt, that the data is enough, and that ROI will be obvious.
- Pricing appears in at least three different forms across the workspace: rough variable formulas, high-ticket pilot/setup models, and later lower monthly SaaS tiers.
- Messaging research discusses WhatsApp, portals, email, SMS, and voice deeply, but the current launch set is still not fully locked in.

## Claims

- Pilot proof remains the dominant business risk. (high) — Founder summary, pitch traction notes
- Pricing is still not fully settled across the material. (high) — Pricing page, older high-ticket pages, newer pitch tiers
- The biggest strategic danger is letting exploratory branches blur the current wedge. (high) — Notion branch analysis across strategy, GGZ, broader outpatient, and current dental pitch
