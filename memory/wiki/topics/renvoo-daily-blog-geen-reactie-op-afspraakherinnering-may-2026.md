# Renvoo Daily Blog: Geen Reactie Op Afspraakherinnering May 2026

## Snapshot

On May 2, 2026, Renvoo’s daily publishing workflow published a new Dutch article focused on `geen reactie op afspraakherinnering in de tandartspraktijk`.

This post gives the blog a more specific operator-facing article inside the appointment-reminders cluster. Instead of restating that reminders alone are not enough, it answers a narrower and more actionable question: what should the clinic do when a reminder receives no response?

## Why This Topic Matters

The Dutch blog already covered broader reminders and confirmation themes. What was still missing was a page for the real operational question that follows silence:

- when does no response become a risk?
- which appointments need earlier attention?
- when should the clinic shift from waiting to following up or rescheduling?

That makes the article useful for both search intent and product positioning.

## What The Article Says

The core argument is that silence after a reminder should trigger clearer workflow decisions rather than passive waiting.

The article breaks that into:

- earlier prioritization of risky appointments
- a fixed follow-up step for silence
- earlier use of rescheduling where appropriate
- faster recovery of freed capacity if the appointment still falls away

It connects that logic directly to daily front-desk workload and schedule fragility.

## Source And Compliance Posture

The article stayed deliberately conservative.

- KNMT and NZa material were used only for wording, policy, and expectation framing.
- The article does not claim measured Renvoo outcomes.
- It does not present policy sources as proof that a certain follow-up sequence guarantees fewer no-shows.
- It passed the repo’s compliance checks before being published.
