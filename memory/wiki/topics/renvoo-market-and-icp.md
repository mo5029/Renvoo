# Renvoo Market And ICP

## Snapshot

Renvoo's clearest current market entry is the Dutch private clinic segment, especially dental clinics. The buyers are operational and financially exposed: clinic owners, practice managers, and operations leaders who directly feel the cost of unused appointment time, manual reminder work, and messy last-minute scheduling.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` names private dental clinics in the Netherlands as the initial wedge.
- The founder summary lists dental, physiotherapy, dermatology, therapy, cosmetic, and outpatient specialty clinics as relevant segments, but treats dental as the strongest short-term wedge.
- `Clinics MVP comparison` and `GGZ as a wedge compared to similar clinics` show active comparison work, meaning wedge choice was tested rather than assumed.
- Later pitch material converges on dental despite those comparisons.

## Claims

- Private dental clinics in the Netherlands are the clearest current market wedge. (high) — Pitch deck 2, Kick off video, founder summary
- The most important buyer roles are clinic owners, practice managers, and operations managers. (high) — Pitch deck 2, founder summary
- GGZ and broader outpatient clinics were explored but are not the cleanest current positioning. (medium) — Clinics MVP comparison, GGZ wedge analysis, later pitch material
