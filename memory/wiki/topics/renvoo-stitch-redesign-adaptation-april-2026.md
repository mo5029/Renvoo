# Renvoo Stitch redesign adaptation April 2026

## Snapshot

Adapted the Stitch redesign export onto the current Renvoo bilingual funnel architecture instead of reviving the older SEO page tree, to preserve live booking, blog, and locale behavior. Used the Stitch export as a design and layout source only, not a truth source. Removed false or unsupported authority framing by replacing the old founder blurb with a founder-led pilot-validation posture, localized shared pricing and download copy for Dutch and English, and kept the product story operational: dental-first, non-clinical scheduling/admin data only, no patient accounts in v1, and pilot-stage honesty. Updated the shared render layer to introduce Stitch-inspired hero stages for home, product, pilot, trust, and blog pages; featured article treatment for the blog index; and local self-hosted Newsreader and Manrope fonts. Updated the main visual system with warm editorial surfaces, stronger two
