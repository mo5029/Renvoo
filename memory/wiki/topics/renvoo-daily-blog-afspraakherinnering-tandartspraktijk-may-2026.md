# Renvoo Daily Blog: Afspraakherinnering Tandartspraktijk May 2026

## Snapshot

On May 7, 2026, Renvoo’s daily publishing workflow published a new Dutch article focused on `afspraakherinnering tandartspraktijk`.

This post adds a more specific operator-facing article inside the appointment-reminders cluster. Instead of only saying that reminders are not enough, it answers a narrower practical question: how should reminder channels, timing, and follow-up actually work together?

## Why This Topic Matters

The Dutch blog already covered confirmation, no-response follow-up, and the limits of reminders in general. What was still missing was the more direct reminder workflow question:

- which role should sms, e-mail, and follow-up each play?
- when should the clinic move from reminding to acting?
- how do reminders connect to rescheduling and recovery?

That makes the article useful for both search intent and product positioning.

## What The Article Says

The core argument is that a reminder is useful, but its real operational value depends on what the clinic does after sending it.

The article breaks that into:

- prioritizing which appointments need earlier certainty
- assigning different roles to channels
- treating silence as a decision point
- connecting reminders to rescheduling and recovery

It keeps the argument practical and avoids pretending one channel recipe is universally best.

## Source And Compliance Posture

The article stayed deliberately conservative.

- KNMT material was used only for wording and communication framing.
- NZa and KNMT policy material were used only for expectations and conditions context.
- The article does not claim measured Renvoo outcomes or universal channel superiority.
