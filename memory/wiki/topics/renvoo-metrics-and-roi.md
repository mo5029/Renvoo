# Renvoo Metrics And ROI

## Snapshot

Renvoo's commercial strength is that its value can be translated into hard clinic outcomes. The current metrics stack is simple and strong: reduce no-shows, recover lost revenue, improve chair utilization, and save staff time. At this stage, the numbers in circulation are mainly scenario math and industry framing, which means the next step is proving them in real pilots.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` names reduction in no-show rate as the North Star Metric.
- The founder summary adds revenue recovered per month, chair utilization, and admin hours saved as the supporting stack.
- `Plan for the short-term` and `GDPR & data` both include target pilot outcomes such as 15-25% no-show reduction, 30%+ backfill within 72 hours, and 20%+ admin time reduction.
- Pitch material repeatedly uses examples such as 2 no-shows per day causing roughly EUR8k monthly losses in dental settings.

## Claims

- The North Star Metric is reduction in no-show rate. (high) — Pitch deck 2, founder summary
- Recovered revenue, utilization, and admin hours saved are the most important supporting metrics. (high) — Pitch deck 2, founder summary
- Most current ROI numbers are sales assumptions or scenario models, not live pilot proof. (high) — Pitch deck 2 traction stage, founder summary
