# Renvoo Roadmap And Proof Plan

## Snapshot

Renvoo's roadmap should be read in phases. First: get access, ship the lightweight workflow, and prove the economics. Second: make integration smoother and deepen clinic trust. Third: expand across more clinics, more locations, and eventually more countries. The highest-value short-term outcome is not a prettier concept deck. It is pilot proof.

## Evidence

- `Kick off video` explicitly names connectors and partner clinics as the current bottleneck.
- `Plan for the short-term` lays out a phased MVP plan around backend, secure patient actions, SMS/email, compliance docs, and measured rollout.
- `Pitch deck 2` sets goals such as deploying MVP to 3 clinics by June and proving measurable no-show reduction.
- `Strategy` expands the long-term company direction toward broader clinic and hospital interactions, but only after the wedge is proven.

## Claims

- The immediate bottleneck is partner-clinic access and integration testing, not conceptual product ideation. (high) — Kick off video, founder summary
- The stated near-term proof target is measurable no-show reduction in 60-90 day pilots. (high) — Kick off video, Plan for the short-term, GDPR & data
- The expansion path is dental first, then multi-clinic and broader European categories later. (high) — Pitch deck 2, Strategy, founder summary
