# Renvoo Company Overview

## Snapshot

Renvoo is being built as a Dutch healthtech startup focused on wasted appointment capacity. Its current sharpest form is not a broad hospital software company and not a generic reminder tool. It is an operational system for predicting likely no-shows, intervening before lost appointments happen, and recovering clinic capacity when cancellations or absences occur. The immediate commercial wedge is private clinics, with dental clinics in the Netherlands serving as the clearest entry point.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` and `Kick off video` both anchor the story around Dutch private dental clinics, no-show reduction, and appointment recovery.
- The founder summary repeatedly frames Renvoo as "less lost revenue, less admin work, fewer empty chairs/slots."
- `Strategy` expands the long-term vision into broader clinic and hospital workflows, but current go-to-market remains much narrower.
- `Renvoo Dashboard` acts as a central hub linking product, strategy, pricing, outreach, and compliance work, indicating a real but still early operating system around the company.

## Claims

- Renvoo is an early-stage, pre-revenue Dutch healthtech startup. (high) — Pitch deck 2, Kick off video, founder summary
- The current narrow wedge is no-show prevention and appointment recovery for private clinics, especially dental clinics. (high) — Pitch deck 2, Kick off video, founder summary
- Renvoo's strongest business framing is operational value creation rather than generic AI. (high) — Pitch deck 2, GTM scripts, founder summary
