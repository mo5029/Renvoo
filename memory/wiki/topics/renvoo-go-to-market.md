# Renvoo Go To Market

## Snapshot

Renvoo's go-to-market approach is founder-led and relationship-heavy. The immediate motion is not broad-scale marketing automation. It is direct selling to clinics, preferably face-to-face, backed by tightly framed outreach that talks about lost revenue, wasted staff time, low-friction onboarding, and GDPR-conscious implementation. Pilot proof is the next real milestone.

## Evidence

- The founder summary explicitly prioritizes face-to-face clinic access, events, introductions, and direct pitches.
- `Email script` and `Cold call script` use operational language: planning pain, no medical data, read-only access, and free pilot framing.
- `Pitch deck 2` still shows `0` discovery calls and `0` active pipeline, which means traction is still a build task rather than an existing advantage.
- `Kick off video` frames connector access and partner clinics as the current bottleneck.

## Claims

- Renvoo is currently sold founder-first rather than through scaled outbound or channel partnerships. (high) — Founder summary, email script, cold call script
- The most effective messaging emphasizes revenue recovery, reduced admin load, and low-friction deployment. (high) — Email script, cold call script, founder summary
- The company still needs real clinic proof more than additional positioning work. (high) — Pitch deck 2 traction slide, founder summary
