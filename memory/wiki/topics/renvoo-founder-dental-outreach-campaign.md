# Renvoo Founder Dental Outreach Campaign

## Snapshot

Renvoo's outreach has moved beyond event planning into a founder-led clinic campaign. The current motion is not a broad spray. It is a ranked dental-first email list with a short founder introduction, a clear operational pain statement, and a 15-minute validation ask.

## What is true now

- The campaign has a verified 100-clinic lead list.
- The message is Dutch-first and founder-led.
- The copy is built around no-shows, late cancellations, confirmations, and rescheduling work.
- The first send wave is already live and the remaining queue is staged for follow-up execution.
- The language preference changed mid-campaign, so the early wave is Dutch and the queued remainder is now English-first.

## Why this matters

- Renvoo needs proof from clinics more than more abstract strategy.
- Founder credibility matters in cold dental outreach because the product is still early.
- Region-first prioritization keeps the campaign aligned with the current wedge instead of drifting into random national outreach.
- The send tracker now makes the next steps operational: follow-up timing, reply handling, and further queue execution.

## Claims

- Renvoo now has a ranked founder campaign file with 100 verified dental clinic email leads. (high) — docs/outreach/founder-dental-top-100.csv
- The ranking starts with core Brainport clinics, then Brabant expansion, then adjacent and backup cities. (high) — docs/outreach/founder-dental-top-100.csv, docs/outreach/founder-dental-campaign-notes.md
- The founder dental message now explicitly uses Mohamed Ibrahim's Team Polar TU/e and NVIDIA 6G background. (high) — docs/outreach/founder-dental-email-template.md
- The founder send wave reached 48 clinics on April 17, 2026, and left 52 queued in the tracker. (high) — docs/outreach/founder-dental-outreach-tracker.csv, Gmail sends
