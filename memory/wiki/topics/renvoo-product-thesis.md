# Renvoo Product Thesis

## Snapshot

Renvoo is built on the idea that no-shows are not just a patient behavior issue. They are a clinic operations problem. The product therefore aims to do more than send reminders. It scores risk using operational data, adapts outreach intensity to that risk, tracks responses centrally, supports earlier rescheduling, and tries to recover otherwise lost capacity through waitlist or short-notice replacement logic.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` defines the solution as predicting and preventing no-shows by automatically confirming, rescheduling, and backfilling appointments.
- The founder summary explicitly says the system should identify risk, trigger the right action, reduce uncertainty, and recover wasted capacity.
- `Realisations` emphasizes selling fewer no-shows and higher realized revenue rather than selling a model.
- `4.Replace` shows that backfill is treated as a dedicated product module.

## Claims

- Renvoo is conceptually stronger as a risk-based operational workflow than as a generic reminder tool. (high) — Founder summary, Pitch deck 2
- The core workflow is predict, intervene, confirm, reschedule, and backfill. (high) — Pitch deck 2, product pages, founder summary
- Backfill is a major part of the long-term value proposition, not a side feature. (high) — Pitch deck 2, Replace page, founder summary
