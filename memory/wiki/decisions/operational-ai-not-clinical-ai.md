# Operational AI Not Clinical AI

## Snapshot

Future reasoning should keep Renvoo in the operational lane. The product is about attendance risk, scheduling certainty, and revenue protection, not treatment decisions or clinical judgment.

- `GDPR & data` makes the non-clinical posture explicit.
- The founder summary emphasizes operational efficiency rather than medical AI.
- Later pitch wording aligns with the same frame.
