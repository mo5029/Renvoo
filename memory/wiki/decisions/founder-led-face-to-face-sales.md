# Founder Led Face To Face Sales

## Snapshot

At Renvoo's current stage, the best GTM default is direct founder-led selling. This is especially true because healthcare trust, workflow nuance, and proof-building all benefit from direct conversations.

## Evidence

- The founder summary explicitly prioritizes face-to-face opportunities and introductions.
- Email and cold-call scripts are written as founder outreach, not generic marketing automation.
