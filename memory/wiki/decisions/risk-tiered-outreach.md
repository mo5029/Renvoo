# Risk Tiered Outreach

## Snapshot

Renvoo should assume a differentiated communication policy: low-risk appointments get lighter-touch reminders, while higher-risk appointments can trigger stronger or earlier intervention.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` explicitly frames intervention around risk tiers.
- `Summary Task 1` and `Findings 8-Nov-2025` go deeper into channel logic and escalation rules.
- The founder summary treats this as core to the system's advantage.

## Claims

- Risk-based intervention is one of Renvoo's main differentiators versus generic reminder software. (high) — Pitch deck 2, founder summary
