# Renvoo

## Snapshot

Renvoo is the company entity around which all notes in this vault are organized. Its current sharpest commercial form is a clinic operations product that predicts likely no-shows, automates patient communication, and helps recover wasted appointment capacity.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` and `Kick off video` provide the cleanest current company narrative.
- `Strategy` broadens the eventual ambition but does not replace the current wedge.
- The founder summary frames Renvoo as an operational layer for fewer no-shows, less admin burden, and more recovered revenue.

## Claims

- Renvoo is currently best understood as a startup focused on no-show prevention and appointment recovery for clinics. (high) — Pitch deck 2, founder summary
- The current wedge is narrow even though the long-term ambition is much broader. (high) — Strategy, founder summary
