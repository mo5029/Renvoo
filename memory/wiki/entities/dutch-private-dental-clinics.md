# Dutch Private Dental Clinics

## Snapshot

This segment is the most concrete market entity in Renvoo's current memory. It combines visible no-show pain, high enough appointment value to make ROI legible, and simpler commercial access than large institutional healthcare buyers.

## Evidence

- `Pitch deck 2` explicitly argues dental is strong because workflows are standardized and decisions are owner-led.
- The founder summary repeatedly calls dental the best short-term wedge even while acknowledging adjacent clinic categories.
- Example ROI numbers in the pitch are overwhelmingly dental-specific.

## Claims

- Dental clinics are the strongest current wedge because they combine recurring appointments, measurable no-show rates, and simple operational ROI. (high) — Pitch deck 2, founder summary
- Renvoo's dental pitch relies on owner-led decision making and shorter sales cycles than hospitals. (high) — Pitch deck 2
