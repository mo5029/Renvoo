- Renvoo is an early-stage Dutch healthtech startup focused on helping clinics reduce no-shows, manage cancellations better, automate confirmation and rescheduling, and potentially backfill empty appointments.
- The sharpest short-term wedge is private clinics, especially dental clinics in the Netherlands.
- The real buyer is operational:

  - practice manager

  - predict risk

  - reduce uncertainty

- The compliance position is GDPR-conscious, low-friction, and read-only where possible.

  - audit log

# Strongest Founder-Level Thesis

Clinics lose money every day because patients miss appointments, cancel too late, and leave valuable treatment slots empty. Staff then waste hours manually chasing confirmations and rescheduling people. Renvoo solves this by predicting no-show risk using administrative scheduling data, automating patient outreach based on risk, improving confirmation and rescheduling workflows, and helping clinics backfill empty slots. The result is fewer no-shows, more recovered revenue, higher chair utilization, and less admin burden.
