# Data Processing Agreement

Template only. Review with Dutch counsel before signature.

## Parties

- Controller: `[Clinic legal name]`
- Processor: `Renvoo B.V.`

## 1. Subject matter and duration

Renvoo processes personal data on behalf of the Clinic for the duration of the pilot or service term described in the Master Services Agreement and Statement of Work.

## 2. Nature and purpose

Purpose of processing:

- appointment confirmation support
- rescheduling support
- no-show risk operations support
- appointment-capacity recovery support
- operational reporting and auditability for the Clinic

## 3. Categories of data subjects

- patients or prospective patients whose appointments are in scope
- clinic staff using the pilot service

## 4. Categories of personal data

Allowed default categories:

- patient name
- clinic patient identifier
- mobile phone and email
- preferred contact channel
- appointment identifiers
- appointment times, status, and type
- practitioner or location assignment
- message status, confirmation status, response status, and cancellation timing

Excluded by default:

- BSN
- diagnosis or diagnostic codes
- medication data
- allergies
- lab data
- clinical notes or free text
- treatment plans

## 5. Processor obligations

Renvoo will:

- process data only on documented controller instructions
- ensure confidentiality obligations for authorized personnel
- implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- engage subprocessors only as listed or later approved
- assist the Clinic with data subject requests, DPIAs, and breach support
- delete or return personal data at the end of services, unless law requires retention
- make information available to demonstrate compliance

## 6. Breach support

Renvoo will notify the Clinic without undue delay after confirming a personal-data breach affecting Clinic data and will provide available facts, likely impact, containment steps, and next actions.

## 7. International transfers

No transfer mechanism may be used unless it is documented in the subprocessor register and approved under the Clinic's instructions and applicable law.

## 8. Subprocessors

The current approved subprocessors are listed in the Security And Subprocessors Annex and processor register.

## 9. Return and deletion

On termination, the Clinic may instruct Renvoo to:

- return Clinic data in an agreed export format
- delete active copies
- confirm backup aging timelines where immediate deletion is not technically possible

## 10. Audit support

Use a reasonable written-notice audit right, limited to relevant controls and without exposing other customer data or security-sensitive internals.
