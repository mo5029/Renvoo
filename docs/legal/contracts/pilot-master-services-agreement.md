# Pilot Master Services Agreement

Template only. Review with Dutch counsel before signature.

## Parties

- `Renvoo B.V.`, a Dutch private limited company, registered in the Netherlands
- `[Clinic legal name]`, a clinic operating in the Netherlands

## 1. Service

Renvoo provides a pilot SaaS service focused on no-show prevention, appointment confirmation, rescheduling support, and appointment-capacity recovery for clinic operations.

The service is operational only. It is not intended to provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, medical advice, or clinical decision support.

## 2. Pilot scope

- Initial scope follows the attached Statement of Work
- Pilot is limited to agreed clinic locations and workflows
- Pilot begins with CSV-first, read-only, or similarly lightweight onboarding unless the parties agree otherwise in writing

## 3. Data roles

- For patient and appointment operational data processed for clinic workflows, the Clinic acts as controller
- Renvoo acts as processor on documented Clinic instructions
- Renvoo acts as controller for its own website, finance, hiring, CRM, and internal business operations data

## 4. Permitted data boundary

The default pilot scope is limited to administrative scheduling and communication data. The parties will not intentionally include diagnoses, medication data, clinical notes, lab data, BSN, or treatment-decision fields in the pilot scope unless a written amendment and fresh legal review approve that change.

## 5. Fees

- Fees and pilot timing are stated in the attached Statement of Work
- Taxes are added where applicable
- Invoices are payable within `[30]` days unless otherwise agreed

## 6. Clinic responsibilities

- Provide accurate operational data within the approved scope
- Keep primary responsibility for patient communications, appointment policies, and lawful basis choices
- Review any pilot outputs before relying on them operationally
- Designate a clinic pilot owner and privacy contact

## 7. Renvoo responsibilities

- Provide the pilot service with reasonable skill and care
- Maintain the security controls described in the Security And Subprocessors Annex
- Notify the Clinic without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting Clinic data
- Support export, return, and deletion obligations at pilot end

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party must keep the other party's confidential information secret and use it only for this agreement.

## 9. Intellectual property

- Renvoo retains ownership of the service, software, workflows, documentation, and pre-existing materials
- Clinic retains ownership of Clinic data and Clinic marks
- Feedback may be used by Renvoo to improve the service, provided Clinic confidential information is protected

## 10. Warranties and disclaimers

- Renvoo warrants that the pilot service will materially conform to the agreed pilot scope
- The Clinic acknowledges that the pilot is an early-stage service intended to validate operational fit and outcomes
- The service does not replace professional medical judgment or clinical workflow ownership

## 11. Liability

Use a negotiated and counsel-reviewed liability cap. As a drafting default, cap direct liability at the fees paid or payable under the pilot in the `[12]` months before the claim, excluding liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 12. Term and termination

- Start date: `[date]`
- Initial term: `[number]` months
- Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within `[30]` days
- Either party may terminate if continuing the pilot becomes legally unsafe or inconsistent with the approved data boundary

## 13. Exit handling

At pilot end, Renvoo will follow the agreed offboarding steps for export, return, deletion, and backup aging, subject to legal retention requirements.

## 14. Governing law

Dutch law applies. Courts in the Netherlands have jurisdiction unless the parties agree to another forum in writing.
