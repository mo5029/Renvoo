# Patient Message Notice

Draft short notice for patient-facing reminders, confirmations, and secure rescheduling links.

## Short version

Your clinic uses Renvoo to help manage appointment confirmations, rescheduling, and recovery of otherwise unused appointment time. Renvoo acts on behalf of your clinic for these operational messages.

## Roles

- Your clinic remains responsible for your care relationship and your patient data as controller
- Renvoo provides the operational workflow as processor for the clinic

## What data is used

Only the limited scheduling and communication data needed for the clinic workflow, such as your name, contact details, appointment time, response status, and message history relevant to the appointment.

## What is not the point of this service

This service is not a patient account, patient portal, diagnosis tool, treatment recommendation system, or medical advice service.

## Questions

- Questions about your appointment, your care, or your privacy rights should first go to your clinic
- Renvoo should only be listed as a service provider contact if the clinic wants that contact path shown
