# Website Privacy Notice

Draft notice for Renvoo's marketing site. Finalize legal entity details before publishing.

## Who this notice is for

This notice covers Renvoo's own website, clinic lead capture, and sales conversations. It does not replace a clinic's own patient privacy notice.

## Product posture

Renvoo is being launched as a B2B clinic SaaS for Dutch private clinics. The website is informational and lead-generation oriented. It is not a patient portal.

## What data Renvoo may collect directly

- name and clinic contact details you submit
- company or clinic information you share with us
- communications you send to Renvoo
- limited technical and security information needed to run the site

## Cookies

Renvoo's default launch posture is no non-essential cookies by default. If that changes, a separate consent flow and cookie notice must be enabled first.

## Why Renvoo uses this data

- to answer clinic inquiries
- to schedule validation or pilot conversations
- to operate and secure the website
- to manage normal business administration

## Legal basis

Drafting default:

- contract or pre-contract steps for clinic inquiries
- legitimate interests for core site security and business administration
- consent only if non-essential cookies or optional marketing flows are added

## Retention

Use the retention matrix in this folder. A simple default is to delete inactive website lead records after 12 months unless there is an ongoing business need.

## Your rights

Visitors may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability where applicable under the GDPR.
