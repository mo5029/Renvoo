# Breach Response SOP

This playbook supports Renvoo's processor obligations during clinic pilots.

## Internal targets

- Open incident record immediately after credible confirmation
- Contain first, then classify
- Notify affected clinic without undue delay
- Working target for clinic notice: within `24` hours of confirmed awareness where practicable

## Steps

1. Detect and contain
   - stop unauthorized access if possible
   - preserve logs and evidence
   - identify affected system and timeframe

2. Classify
   - confirm whether personal data is affected
   - determine whether the data is clinic-controller data or Renvoo-controller data
   - estimate scope and likely impact

3. Notify internally
   - founder / incident owner
   - technical owner
   - clinic account owner

4. Notify the clinic
   - what happened
   - when it started or was discovered
   - what data appears affected
   - what has been contained
   - what help the clinic needs for its own controller-side assessment

5. Support controller obligations
   - provide updates fast enough to help the clinic meet the AP `72` hour timeline where relevant
   - document facts, containment, and follow-up

6. Close and improve
   - root cause
   - corrective actions
   - date of closure
   - lessons learned

## Do not do

- do not wait for a perfect technical picture before escalating
- do not let a processor-side team contact patients directly unless the clinic has explicitly authorized that path in writing
