# Renvoo B.V. Setup Checklist

Use this checklist for the simple single-BV structure approved for v1.

## Entity decision

- Operating entity: `Renvoo B.V.`
- Structure: single Dutch BV for now
- Deferred: holding BV, option pool structure, foreign entities

## Pre-notary inputs

- Confirm founders, directors, and initial shareholders
- Confirm share split and voting rights
- Confirm founder vesting or repurchase rules
- Confirm company purpose language stays operational and non-clinical
- Confirm registered address, trade name, and business description
- Run trade name and trademark clearance on `Renvoo`

## Notary package

- Passport / ID copies for founders and directors
- Residential address details
- Draft articles of association
- Share capital and share classes
- Board appointment details
- UBO information for KVK filing

## Immediate post-incorporation actions

- Open business bank account
- Register directors and UBOs with KVK
- Confirm VAT and tax onboarding with Belastingdienst
- Set up bookkeeping and invoice process
- Set up board and shareholder resolution folder
- Calendar annual accounts, VAT, payroll, and corporate filing dates

## Risk controls

- Do not sign clinic contracts personally once the BV exists
- If anything must be signed before incorporation, mark it clearly as `BV i.o.`
- Move all code, domains, decks, and brand assets into BV ownership
- Use the IP assignment templates in this folder before any contractor or founder work continues
