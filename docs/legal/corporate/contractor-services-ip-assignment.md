The Contractor may access clinic-related data only on documented Renvoo instructions and only for the approved service scope.

All software, code, documentation, designs, prompts, analyses, decks, models, and other deliverables created or supplied under the services vest in and are hereby assigned to Renvoo B.V. on creation.

### Security baseline

- MFA on all accounts used for Renvoo work
- No use of personal cloud storage without written approval
- No export of clinic data outside approved systems
- Immediate reporting of suspected incidents or unauthorized access

### Return and deletion

On request or termination, the Contractor must return or securely delete Renvoo and clinic materials, except where law requires retention.
