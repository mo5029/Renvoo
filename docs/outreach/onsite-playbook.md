# On-Site Playbook

Use this at every event you attend.

## 15-Second Openers

### Direct clinic room

Renvoo helps appointment-heavy clinics lose less revenue to no-shows, late cancellations, and empty slots without adding front-desk work.

### Connector room

Renvoo is validating a workflow that predicts no-show risk and improves patient communication for appointment-heavy clinics. I am trying to meet the operators who feel this pain most acutely.

## 30-Second Pitch

Renvoo helps clinics reduce no-shows and recover empty appointment capacity. The product uses non-clinical scheduling and admin data to identify risk, trigger the right confirmation or rescheduling workflow, and make it easier for staff to keep the schedule full without extra manual chasing.

## The Only 3 Qualification Questions

1. How often do late cancellations or no-shows hurt your week?
2. Who currently handles reminders and rescheduling?
3. If you could fix one thing first, would it be fewer no-shows, earlier cancellations, or easier backfill?

## What Counts As A Good Lead

### A lead

- Owner, practice manager, operations manager, or network operator
- Admits current pain
- Accepts a concrete next step

### B lead

- Not the buyer, but clearly understands the pain
- Offers an intro or asks for a deck

### C lead

- General interest only
- No urgency and no next step

## Allowed Next Steps

- Pilot call
- Intro to owner or manager
- Follow-up deck
- Workflow review
- Admin pain interview

## What Not To Do

- Do not lead with AI models or prediction accuracy.
- Do not ask for a product demo on the floor.
- Do not pitch broad hospital infrastructure.
- Do not make the first conversation about integrations.
