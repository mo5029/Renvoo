# Target Building Plan

This file explains how to actually hit the list-building goals behind the outreach sprint without inventing attendee lists that are not public.

## Target Volumes

- `40` direct clinic prospects
- `20` connector prospects
- `15` premium remote conference prospects

## 1. Direct Clinic Prospects: Goal 40

Because there is no strong remaining dental trade event in this exact window, direct-clinic targeting has to be built primarily through direct practice outreach and trusted association routes.

### Direct clinic quota

- `20` dental practice owner or manager targets
- `10` mondzorg or oral-care practice targets
- `10` referral spillover targets from association and connector routes

### Dental practice target mix

- `10` practice owners
- `7` practice managers or operations leads
- `3` multi-location operators or group-level contacts

### Oral-care target mix

- `5` mondhygiënist practice owners
- `3` oral-care practice managers
- `2` referral-capable association or network contacts

### How to source them

1. Use organizer-side confirmation first to make sure you can physically enter the event.
2. Use KNMT and NVM-mondhygiënisten as trusted routes into dental and oral-care practice operators.
3. Search public practice sites, leadership pages, and network pages for owner and manager targets.
4. Use `onsite-playbook.md` at connector events and treat every strong introduction as a target capture moment.
5. Log every relevant person immediately in `outreach-tracker.csv`.
6. Within 24 hours, move strong contacts into a concrete next step.

## 2. Connector Prospects: Goal 20

These are not direct clinic buyers. They are people who can introduce Renvoo to buyers or pilot environments.

### Connector quota

- `5` organizer or association contacts from `contact-routes.csv`
- `5` ecosystem operators from MedtechMeeting and 4TU.Health
- `5` innovation leads or implementation managers from the AI conference ecosystem
- `5` warm referrals created by the first 15 connector conversations

### What counts as a connector

- Healthcare innovation manager
- Association lead
- Ecosystem broker
- Programme manager
- Transformation lead
- Founder-support or valorisation contact with healthcare reach

## 3. Premium Remote Prospects: Goal 15

This list is already prebuilt in `remote-conference-targets.csv`.

### How to work the list

1. Send first touches between `2026-04-28` and `2026-05-02`.
2. Send second touches between `2026-05-05` and `2026-05-07`.
3. Ask for short post-event conversations, not conference meetings.
4. Use them for intros and positioning feedback, not sales pushes.

## Qualification Rules

Add someone to the live list only if at least one of these is true:

- They own the empty-slot or no-show problem.
- They can introduce you to someone who owns it.
- They influence operational workflow, digital transformation, or patient communication.

## Disqualification Rules

Do not spend further time if:

- They are purely academic with no operator reach.
- They like the idea but cannot name a relevant operator.
- They sit in a member-governance room with no practical introduction path.

## Minimum Standard

By the end of each week, your list should include:

- A current owner for every target
- A next step
- A follow-up date
- A one-line note explaining why the target matters
