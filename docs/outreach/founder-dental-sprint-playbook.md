# Renvoo 10-In-10 Dental Booking Sprint

## Goal

Book `10` qualified validation meetings with dental clinic owners, practice managers, or equivalent operators between `2026-04-18` and `2026-04-27`.

## Priority Order

1. Brabant dental clinics already in the founder tracker
2. Nearby southern clinics already in the founder tracker
3. High-confidence reserve clinics from the qualified-email pool
4. Warm-intro routes through associations and connector networks

## Bucket System

### Bucket A: Immediate Conversion

- All clinics already emailed
- Focus on response handling, referrals, and scheduled follow-ups
- No first-touch resend

### Bucket B: High-Confidence New First Touches

- Unsent clinics with direct clinic-domain emails
- Prefer named owner or manager when available
- Every lead needs a score, tier, and one-line pain hypothesis before send

### Bucket C: Expansion Reserve

- Next best leads after Bucket B is exhausted
- Only use once A-tier active leads are fully worked

## Sequence

### Day 0

- verify lead
- assign score and tier
- identify buyer role
- send first email

### Day 1

- LinkedIn connection request or lightweight LinkedIn touch

### Day 3

- follow-up 1 email

### Day 5

- intro request through any credible connector route

### Day 7

- LinkedIn DM if connected, otherwise follow-up 2 email

### Day 9

- final bump

### Day 10

- classify lead as `booked`, `warm`, or `parked`

## Response SLA

- positive reply: respond within `2` hours during working time
- referral: reach the referred contact within `24` hours
- soft no: one respectful future-open loop, then park
- hard no: park immediately
- auto-response: note it and keep the planned sequence

## Daily Minimum

- `6` new first touches
- `4` LinkedIn actions
- `4` follow-ups
- `2` intro requests or referral chases
- same-day tracker cleanup

## Meeting Objective

The validation meeting is not a product demo.

It should confirm:

- how the clinic currently handles no-shows and late cancellations
- who owns reminders, confirmations, and rescheduling
- where the admin burden is highest
- whether empty chair time is a real financial problem

## Meeting Booking Rule

Always offer `2` or `3` concrete time slots in the first reply that moves toward a meeting.

## One-Pager Rule

Send the one-pager only after:

- a positive reply
- a referral to the right person
- a request for more context
- a meeting is close to being booked
