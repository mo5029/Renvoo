# Renvoo Talking Points

## 30-Second Version

Renvoo is a B2B clinic SaaS for Dutch dental clinics. It helps reduce no-shows, handle late cancellations earlier, and recover lost appointment capacity without adding front-desk work. The problem is not just reminders. It is the operational cost of empty chair time, late notice, and manual recovery work. We are starting with dental clinics because the revenue impact is direct, the scheduling pain is clear, and the workflow is easier to validate in practice.

## 2-Minute Version

Dental clinics lose money in two ways when appointment attendance becomes uncertain. First, no-shows and late cancellations leave chair time empty, and that time is hard to recover on short notice. Second, staff spend more time chasing confirmations, rescheduling patients, and trying to fill gaps manually.

Most clinics already send reminders, but reminders alone do not solve operational uncertainty. A sent message is not the same as a confirmed patient, and generic reminder flows treat every appointment the same even when some slots are much riskier than others.

Renvoo is being built as a clinic operations layer for that gap. It is designed to identify higher-risk appointments, support earlier confirmation or rescheduling, and improve the clinic's ability to recover otherwise lost capacity. It is deliberately framed around non-clinical scheduling and communication data, so it fits operational workflows without needing diagnoses, clinical notes, or treatment decisions. It is also deliberately being kept simple in v1: no patient accounts, no public marketplace behavior, and no patient-facing subscription product.

Preliminary pilot pricing starts at EUR 2.49 per appointment, plus a fixed fee depending on the clinic. Final pricing is still being shaped with pilot partners. Right now, the goal is a short validation meeting with a dental clinic owner or practice manager to review the current no-show and rescheduling workflow.

## Top 5 Clinic Pain Points

1. Empty chair time turns booked capacity into lost revenue.
2. Late cancellations still leave too little time to recover the slot.
3. Generic reminders do not show which appointments are truly at risk.
4. Front-desk teams absorb the uncertainty through manual confirmation and rescheduling work.
5. Waiting-list and backfill efforts are valuable but exhausting when they are handled manually.

## Top 3 Renvoo Solution Points

1. Renvoo is designed to help clinics act earlier around no-show risk instead of reacting after a slot is already lost.
2. Renvoo is built around operational workflows like confirmation, rescheduling, and capacity recovery, not clinical decision-making.
3. Renvoo is designed for low-friction adoption through non-clinical data boundaries, lightweight onboarding paths, and secure links instead of patient accounts in v1.

## Objection Handling

### We already send reminders

That makes sense, and reminders are part of the baseline. The gap Renvoo is focused on is what happens when reminders are not enough: no response, late notice, and the manual work needed to recover capacity before the chair time is lost.

### We do not want another system

That is exactly why the current direction is low-friction. The goal is not to force a heavy system change, but to fit around current scheduling operations with lightweight onboarding and operational visibility.

### We are careful with patient data

That caution is right. Renvoo is being designed around non-clinical administrative scheduling and communication data, not diagnoses, clinical notes, or treatment decisions. The aim is operational improvement with narrower data exposure.

### Do patients need to create an account?

No. The current v1 posture is to keep Renvoo as clinic SaaS, not a patient platform. Patient interactions should happen through clinic-triggered reminders, confirmations, and secure links without introducing a separate patient account unless a later phase clearly requires it.

### We are too busy to change workflow

That is part of the problem Renvoo is trying to solve. The point of a short validation meeting is not to introduce a full rollout. It is to understand the current workflow and see whether there is enough operational pain to justify a lightweight pilot.

### How do we know this is worth it?

That is the key question. The current case is based on clear operational logic and scenario math, but the point of the pilot stage is to validate real no-show reduction, recovered revenue, and reduced admin burden with actual clinic workflows.
