# Renvoo Clinic Deck Narrative Plan

- Audience: dental clinic owners, practice managers, and operations-minded leads
- Objective: make the cost of no-shows and late cancellations feel urgent, then present Renvoo as a low-friction operational response
- Story split: `70%` problem, `20%` solution, `10%` cost
- Visual system: white background, teal and dark teal brand system, rounded cards, scheduling motifs, clean data panels
- Slide order:
  1. cover
  2. no-show cost
  3. late-cancellation cost
  4. reminders vs prevention
  5. admin burden
  6. why dental
  7. operational flow
  8. low-friction design
  9. pricing
  10. founder and pilot ask
