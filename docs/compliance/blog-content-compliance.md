# Renvoo Blog Content Compliance

This policy is a hard constraint for all generated blog content, SEO content, automation outputs, and related written materials in this repository.

If any draft violates these rules, it must be rewritten or rejected. Optimization goals do not override this policy.

## Prohibited Content

### 1. False, exaggerated, or unverified claims

Do not generate:

- fake statistics
- guaranteed results
- inflated numbers
- vague claims presented as facts

If data is uncertain, say so clearly or omit it.

### 2. Fake authority

Do not generate:

- fake studies, fake sources, or fake citations
- fake testimonials
- fake case studies
- language that presents something as proven when it is not proven

### 3. Manipulative persuasion

Do not use:

- fear-based pressure
- urgency manipulation
- psychological exploitation
- dark patterns

### 4. Misleading framing

Do not use:

- clickbait
- titles that do not match the content
- vague promises
- hidden conditions

### 5. Haram or questionable practices

Do not promote or normalize:

- interest-based systems (`riba`)
- gambling-like mechanics
- unethical or exploitative business practices

### 6. Inappropriate content

Do not include:

- sexualized language
- offensive language
- disrespectful tone

### 7. Misuse of data

Do not suggest:

- unethical tracking
- privacy violations
- misuse of clinic or patient data
- anything that ignores ownership, consent, or clear boundaries

### 8. Plagiarism

All content must be original.

- do not copy existing material
- if an idea is inspired by a known concept, rewrite it fully in original language

### 9. Uncertain information presented as fact

- clearly distinguish facts, estimates, and opinions
- if unsure, say so
- prefer omission over hallucination

### 10. SEO over truth

SEO must not override honesty.

Do not optimize purely for clicks at the expense of accuracy, fairness, or reader benefit.

## Required Behavior

- prioritize truthfulness, clarity, and benefit to the reader
- use cautious language when certainty is limited
- focus on practical, useful insights for clinic operators
- keep content respectful, ethical, and professionally useful

## Mandatory Pre-Save Questions

Before any blog is saved:

1. Are all claims verifiable or clearly framed as estimates?
2. Is the title accurate and non-misleading?
3. Is there any exaggeration or manipulation?
4. Would this content be considered honest and fair?

If the answer is no to any of the above, rewrite or reject the draft.
