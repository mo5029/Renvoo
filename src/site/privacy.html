Website privacy note

This page describes the simple privacy posture planned for Renvoo's clinic-SaaS marketing site and pilot outreach flow. It is intentionally narrow and should be finalized with the formal `Renvoo B.V.` legal contact details before launch.

What this page covers

This note covers Renvoo's own website, clinic lead capture, and business-development communications. It does not replace a clinic's own patient privacy notice.

Current product posture

  • Renvoo is being launched as B2B clinic SaaS for Dutch private clinics.
  • The site is informational and lead-generation oriented.
  • The site is not a patient portal or patient account product.

Data Renvoo may collect directly

  • name, clinic, and business contact details you send us
  • messages, meeting requests, and pilot discussion notes
  • basic technical and security information needed to operate the site

Cookies

Renvoo's planned default launch posture is no non-essential cookies by default. If that changes, an explicit consent flow should be added first.

Why the data is used

  • to answer clinic inquiries
  • to schedule validation or pilot conversations
  • to operate and secure the website
  • to manage normal business administration

Important implementation note

Before public launch, replace this pre-launch note with the finalized website privacy notice from the legal pack and add `Renvoo B.V.` contact details, retention choices, and any approved vendor list.

Renvoo keeps its launch privacy posture intentionally narrow.
